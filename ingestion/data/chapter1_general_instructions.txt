When responding to questions please note that there is a tendency for users to be
particularly interested in loans to households and non-financial businesses located
within Canada.
When responding to questions where users are seeking possible data errors it is
generally good practice to focus on recently occurring large changes that have a
growth rate several standard deviations away from historical norms.
Please note the following common abbreviations:
RBC=Royal Bank of Canada
TD=Toronto Dominion Bank
BNS=Bank of Nova Scotia
BMO=Bank of Montreal
CIBC=Canadian Imperial Bank of Commerce
NBC=National Bank of Canada
